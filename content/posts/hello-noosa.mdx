# Hello, Noosa — from the new foundation

This is the first post on the new **My Noosa Heads** foundation.
We are rebuilding the site on a modern stack so we can publish honestly,
quickly, and at the level of detail the destination deserves.

## Why we are rebuilding

The previous build served us well, but:

- Content was locked in JSX pages — slow to update, hard to review.
- Design and content were tangled — small copy changes required redeploys.
- The custom domain was parked at the registrar with no clean path to flip.

The new foundation fixes all three.

## What is shipping in Sprint 1.1

Sprint 1.1 is a **foundation scaffold only**. No content yet — that lands in
Sprint 1.3. In this sprint:

- Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript strict + Tailwind 3 + MDX
- 7-token coastal palette exposed as CSS custom properties
- Vercel auto-deploy wired on `main`
- Production URL serving 200 OK
- A single landing page: "Hello, Noosa — coming soon"

## What comes next

1. **Sprint 1.2** — Design system (typography, component library, accessibility).
2. **Sprint 1.3** — Content (where to stay, eat & drink, surf & weather, hikes, things to do, itineraries) + the custom domain flip.

We will update this post when each sprint ships.

> Honest, sourced, current information about Noosa Heads — coming soon.
